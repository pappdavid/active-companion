# Acme Corp — account notes

## Background
Acme Corp is a mid-size logistics company evaluating our platform for their
operations team. They currently use AcmeSoft for reporting and are frustrated
with slow dashboards.

## Pricing agreed with similar accounts
For teams around twenty seats we typically quote forty dollars per seat per
month with a fifteen percent discount on annual commitments. Pilots run 30 days
free including onboarding.

## Competitive positioning vs AcmeSoft
Unlike AcmeSoft, we include unlimited API integrations at no extra charge.
Customers who switched from AcmeSoft report sixty percent faster reporting and
a migration handled by a dedicated engineer with zero downtime.

## Migration risk handling
Migrations use a parallel run period so there is no downtime. Our record is
ninety eight percent of migrations completed without any interruption. A
dedicated solutions engineer is assigned for the full cutover window.
