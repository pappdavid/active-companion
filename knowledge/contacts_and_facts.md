## Ingestion platform facts
The ingestion API is a batched HTTP endpoint with webhook callbacks. Sustained
throughput is one hundred thousand events per second with end to end latency
under two hundred milliseconds. All webhook endpoints require SSL and
certificates rotate automatically every ninety days.

## Outage compensation policy
For verified outages longer than one hour we offer service credits of ten
percent of the monthly fee per affected hour, capped at fifty percent. A
written root cause summary is delivered within twenty four hours.
